# The Long Dimming expansion

The playable campaign grows to eight acts and twenty-four main quests. Its central mystery changes meaning across the journey: the darkness began as protection, the keeper's lantern carries a stolen memory, and restoring sunlight may destroy the lives built inside that protection. Major decisions remain in the save and alter later dialogue, access to solutions, and one of three endings.

Main objectives alternate between guardians, investigations, ritual preparation, rescue, and conversations in town. Side stories belong to people with individual motives; their outcomes affect supplies, trust, and later choices. Future plot details stay hidden until a quest becomes available.

Expeditions grow from thirty-three to sixty-three nodes with seeded variation, region-specific events, puzzles, hazards, refuges, and discoverable shortcuts. Critical objectives always have reachable nodes. Old saves remain readable, including seventeen-node expeditions.

All forty-eight equipment pieces receive original generated art, arranged in three sixteen-item atlases. Story, region, and enemy atlases expand the illustrated world. All humanoid faces remain concealed.

An eight-page opening, a practical introduction, and a contextual journey guide replace the old repetitive guidance. Combat receives action-specific sound, impact animation, damage feedback, and boss mechanics. Ambient sound uses wind, rainfall, water, insects, and distant environmental textures without instrumental music.

Verification will cover content references, prerequisites, reachable campaign objectives, endings, old-save migration, dungeon connectivity, finite resources, a complete expanded campaign, web/mobile layout, native packaging, and the updated signed APK.
