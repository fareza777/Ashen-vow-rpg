Ashen Vow RPG

Privacy Policy · Effective October 7, 2026

Ashen Vow RPG is an offline-first, single-player dark fantasy role-playing game. This policy explains what the app stores and what third-party services may process when optional Android features are enabled.

Information stored by the game

The game does not require an account, sign-in, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location. Character progress, settings, accessibility choices, and local saves are stored on the device using local app storage. The game does not send those saves to a game server.

Advertising

The Android build can show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The release currently uses Google’s test ad units while the store listing is being prepared. When advertising is enabled, Google may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction information according to its own policies and the choices available in the Google ad consent flow.

Ad settings and any available privacy choices can be managed in the app’s Settings screen and through Google’s Android advertising controls. Google’s policies are available at Google partner-site policies.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads product is a one-time Google Play purchase. Ashen Vow RPG does not receive or store payment card details. Google Play processes payment, purchase status, and refunds. The app stores only the local entitlement needed to suppress ads after Google Play confirms ownership.

Sharing and external links

The Share and Rate actions open the Android share sheet or Google Play. Those services handle any information you choose to share or submit. The game does not sell personal information.

Children

Ashen Vow RPG is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its third-party services change. The effective date above will be updated with material revisions. Questions can be raised through the project repository at github.com/fareza777/Ashen-vow-rpg/issues.
