# Ashen Vow — English store creative

Version shown: 0.2.9. Original title: Ashen Vow: The Hollow Below.

## Direction

A light that remembers. Charcoal, bone, aged brass and restrained ember red.
An original lantern relic anchors the icon; the ruined sanctuary anchors the banner.
Human faces are always concealed or turned away. No green cast, faces in the icon,
rankings, testimonials, store badges, invented features or release claims.

## Eight phone screenshots

1. Every turn has a cost — turn-based combat.
2. The dark offers a choice — branching dungeon events.
3. No descent is the same — generated routes and concealed rooms.
4. Your name. Tomorrow's date. — illustrated quest narrative.
5. Forge your own oath — attributes and three skill disciplines.
6. Relics with a past — illustrated equipment and build choices.
7. Follow more than one story — main and side quests.
8. Return. Rebuild. Descend. — Vesper's Rest, the core loop and local saves.

Capture the real React game in a separate, disposable browser origin. Fixture saves
use actual game data and the real combat reducer. No alterations to the player's
existing save. Do not fabricate gameplay interfaces for advertising.

## Portrait trailer

30 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Strong hook in the first three seconds. Real
gameplay is visible immediately and throughout at least 80% of the timeline.
Legible English captions work with muted autoplay. Close with the title and
the theme of carrying a light. Sound design uses ambience, metal, impacts,
paper and breath; no instrumental music.

Hook: “The bell has your name. Its date is tomorrow.”

Arc: survival → unknown routes → meaningful choices → build identity → equipment
→ a wider story → return and descend → Ashen Vow.

## Platform reference

Google Play official preview-asset requirements checked 6 October 2026:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en

Icon: 512 × 512, PNG, at most 1024 KB. Feature graphic: 1024 × 500, RGB PNG.
Phone screenshots: eight 1080 × 1920 RGB PNGs. Headlines occupy under 20% of
each screenshot. The first three prioritize actual gameplay. Video delivered
as MP4; Google Play's preview field takes a public or unlisted, embeddable
YouTube URL rather than the local MP4 file.
